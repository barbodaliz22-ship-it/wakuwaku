The Concept

A restaurant drawn, not decorated. Four months, one artist, zero colour.

01 — Interior

Ink on every surface

Monochrome linework covers floor to ceiling — a hand-drawn homage to Vermeer, Munch and The Last Supper, recast with cats in sunglasses.

02 — Kitchen

Far East, plated bold

Omurice under a whole glazed eel. Ramen burgers. Dishes built to be the one splash of colour in a black-and-white room.

03 — Atmosphere

Step into the panel

You're not seated at a table. You're dropped into a comic strip mid-scene — and the food is the punchline.
